# Airplane Wing Shape and Lift

[All projects](../../README.md) · [Project home](README.md) · [Portfolio](https://github.com/MadeWithoutCaffeine/Michael-Polanczyk-Portfolio)

In eighth grade, I investigated how airplane-wing shape affects lift. It was a chance to take my interest in aircraft and turn it into a science fair experiment.

## Background

I wanted to investigate a question about flight. I presented the project at my local science fair and at the SEMO science fair.

## What I want to explore next

I would like to revisit the question with a small wind tunnel, interchangeable wing models, and more repeatable measurements. I could use the CAD, 3D printing, and programming experience I have gained since the original project.
