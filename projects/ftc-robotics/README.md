## My contribution

I develop and maintain the robot controls, design custom parts and assemblies in Inventor, and handle printing and part production. I also contribute to assembly and troubleshooting.

Working across these areas means checking how the design, manufactured parts, and software work together. I help teammates learn parts of the process so more people can contribute to the technical work.

## Teaching and future work

I want to build on the help I already give teammates by teaching younger students introductory programming, CAD, and 3D printing. The goal is to help them take on parts of the robot and keep those skills on the team.

The linked programming collection is a teaching reference. Its examples are separate from verified competition code; compilation and physical robot checks remain pending as recorded in the guides.
