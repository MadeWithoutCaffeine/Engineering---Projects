## Measurements and observations

The bridge weighed **28.65 g** and supported **13.2 lb**. Converting both values to grams gives a load-to-mass ratio of approximately **209:1**. This is calculated from the recorded measurements rather than an official score.

My earlier estimate of around 90% efficiency was a personal expectation. The [Results page](results.md) keeps that estimate separate from the measured load.

The account of the strings loosening and slipping comes from my experience of the test and inspecting the returned bridge. The photographs show its visible condition; they don't establish how much more weight it could have held.

## Scholarship interests

MoDOT's 2026 recap says Missouri S&T and Southeast Missouri State University awarded scholarships to top winners. I'm interested in future opportunities connected to the competition as I work toward attending S&T.

The next competition's award terms and eligible majors would need to be confirmed before I could know whether an award would apply to my plans.

## What I want to take into next year's build

I want to start earlier and compare joints, member sizes, string anchors, and tensioning methods through physical tests. Having extra material for small experiments would help me make those choices before building the final bridge.
