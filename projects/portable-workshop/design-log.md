## Starting with the space I need

The idea came from needing more room than I have in our shared home office. I want somewhere to leave a project set up, keep my printing equipment together, and have a desk for CAD and programming.

The layout has an open main workshop and an office separated by a sliding door. That gives me the option to use them together or close off the office.

## Rethinking the design

ChatGPT provided example images to help visualize early ideas. As I worked on the model, I needed more research and went back through the geometry and framing many times. I made my own decisions about the dimensions, frame design, and how to keep portability part of the plan.

Heating, insulation, doors, and windows also affect how useful the space will be. I'm considering those alongside the framing and layout instead of waiting until the shell is built.

## November 12, 2026 — Current revision

The [current CAD photo](README.md#current-cad-model) shows the floor and wall framing, openings, and a roof assembly displayed separately above the walls. It records where the design stands now; there are still things I need to adjust.

I'm using CAD to work out the vision before spending money on materials. The next work is refining the framing and fit, checking the office layout, and developing the materials and cost plan.

The printing area will need room for my P2S and AMS 2 Pro, supplies, and a future filament dryer. I want the dryer to be useful for long-term storage while keeping electricity use low. I also need to decide whether to move my desktop into the office or build a separate setup.
