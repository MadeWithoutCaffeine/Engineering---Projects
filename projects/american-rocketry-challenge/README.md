## Background

I've wanted to get more involved in rocketry, and this gives me a reason to start working on it with other people. I already spend a lot of time on CAD, programming, and making parts through robotics and my own projects. I'd like to bring that experience into our work while learning more about rocket design.

What interests me is having a specific challenge to design around. A design can look good on paper, but we would still need to find out how it performs and what needs to change.

## Current status

We are coming up with designs and discussing ideas with our instructor. We haven't settled on a final design yet. This page records the start of that work; we are still working toward entering the competition.

Our individual responsibilities are still being worked out. I want this to be a team effort, with each of us contributing to the design and understanding the decisions we make.

## Rough approach

We plan to start by reviewing the current challenge requirements and comparing our ideas against them. From there, I would like us to develop a design, review its expected flight, and make changes based on what we learn from testing.

I want to keep our sketches, CAD revisions, predictions, and eventual flight records together so we can compare what we expected with what actually happened. Those records could also help us identify a research question for a possible science fair project.
