The PC is assembled and in use. I replaced the original Arc A770 with an MSI RTX 3080 Gaming Z Trio LHR and kept the A770.

I haven't carried out a controlled benchmark or temperature comparison, so I don't have measured performance gains to report. I'd like to record those under repeatable conditions.
